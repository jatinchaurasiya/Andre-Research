- Icon psychology: research what top icons in this category look like, then differentiate.

DOWNLOAD VELOCITY TACTICS (first 30 days):
The first 72 hours after launch determine your Play Store ranking for weeks.
- Pre-launch waitlist → all sign up day 1.
- Coordinated Reddit posts across multiple relevant subreddits (same day, different posts).
- ProductHunt + Hacker News Show HN on same day.
- Personal network mobilisation playbook.
- "Hunting" strategy — get a PH hunter with 500+ followers to post it.

VIRAL LOOP (built into the app):
Design a referral mechanic that unlocks value — a feature, extra capacity, or vanity item — when a user shares with 2 friends. One tap to share, deep link brings the recipient directly to the right screen.

MONETISATION MARKETING STRATEGY:

### Free-to-Paid Conversion Playbook
- App Store listing must NOT lead with price — lead with the free value.
- Screenshots must show the free tier looking great (not crippled).
- One screenshot dedicated to Premium — show the upgrade as aspiration.
- In-app, the upgrade prompt copy that converts best:
  "You've [completed X tasks] — unlock unlimited for $1.99/month."
  Never: "You've hit your limit. Upgrade to continue."

### Ad Revenue Optimisation (Organic)
- Higher DAU = higher ad revenue (obvious but: state the DAU target needed for meaningful ad revenue in this category).
- Use the eCPM number you researched above to estimate revenue per 1000 impressions.
- Mediation: AdMob supports ad network mediation — list the top 2 networks to add after launch to increase eCPM (all free to integrate).

### Rewarded Ad Virality Angle
- "Share to unlock" + "Watch to unlock" — both drive organic growth.
- In marketing copy: "Free forever. No forced upgrades. Just watch an optional ad to unlock [premium feature] whenever you want." This framing INCREASES conversion and positive reviews.
